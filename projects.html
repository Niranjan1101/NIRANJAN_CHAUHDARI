Academic projects

Projects

Five projects from my diploma and degree studies, covering renewable energy, EV infrastructure, IoT monitoring and power line protection.

Renewable energy · Material handling

Renewable Energy Based Screw and Belt Conveyor

Design and development of an electrically operated screw and belt conveyor powered by renewable energy.

Objective: improve material handling efficiency and reduce manual effort.

EV · Electrical safety

EV Charging Station Planning

Planning and installation study for an electric vehicle charging station.

Focus: power requirements, electrical safety and proper system layout.

IoT · Protection

IoT-Based Smart Substation

Remote monitoring and protection of a substation by sensing voltage, current and temperature and sending live data over IoT.

Objective: fault detection, alerts and less manual inspection.

IoT · Motors

IoT-Based Motor Temperature Monitoring System

A system that monitors the temperature of a motor and makes the readings available remotely over IoT.

Focus: early warning of overheating and less manual checking.

Transmission · Fault detection

Transmission Tower Tilt and Conductor Break Detection System

A detection system for transmission lines that senses tower tilt and conductor breaks.

Focus: spotting line faults quickly and reducing manual line patrolling.
